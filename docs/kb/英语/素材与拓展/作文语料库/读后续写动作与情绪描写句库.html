读后续写动作与情绪描写句库（作文语料库 · 只增不减）

用途：读后续写（广东新高考题型，2025.10 真光卷已考）的描写弹药库。读后续写的得分核心是情节线 + 情感线双线并行：每推进一步情节，配一层情绪变化。 用法：先读原文抓"情节锚点"（人物/冲突/伏笔），续写时按"情绪 → 动作 → 对话 → 升华"四件套组织每段；描写句从本库挑用，避免重复 same words（happy/sad 全文满天飞是最大扣分点）。

一、情节-情感双线法（方法框架）

  1. 划原文锚点：开头三要素（人物关系/时间地点/核心冲突）+ 结尾两段首句（已给，决定续写方向）。
  2. 设计双线：情节线（要解决什么）+ 情感线（从负到正的弧线：失望→希望→温暖→升华）。
  3. 每段五句式：情绪描写 1 + 动作描写 2 + 对话 1 + 环境呼应 1。
  4. 收尾升华：点题句（呼应首段的爱/成长/善意主题），不写新冲突。

二、情绪描写句库（按弧线阶段分区）

失望/焦虑段（故事开端）

  • A wave of disappointment washed over him.
  • Her heart sank / heavy with sorrow, she stared at...
  • He hung his head, tears welling up in his eyes.
  • Anxious and helpless, the boy kept asking where everyone was.（真光卷原文句式）

希望/转机段

  • A flicker of hope lit up her face.
  • Much to his relief, ...
  • His eyes widened with excitement / sparkled with hope.（真光卷 sparkling eyes）
  • At that moment, warmth flooded through her heart.

温暖/成功段（故事收尾）

  • Overwhelmed with joy, she could hardly say a word.
  • His face beamed / lit up with a broad smile.
  • Tears of joy trickled down her cheeks.
  • A sense of achievement and gratitude filled the air.

三、动作描写句库（替换 said/walked/looked）

替换 said（对话标签）

平淡 升级
said whispered / murmured（低语）
said announced / declared（宣布）
said excitedly exclaimed / chimed in（欢呼插话）
asked inquired, tilting her head（歪头询问）

替换 walked / ran

  • He dashed / raced toward...（真光卷 rushed to a hospital）
  • She tiptoed to the door, holding her breath.
  • They flocked to the beach, cheering and waving.（真光卷续写场景）
  • He stumbled forward, gasping for breath.

替换 looked

  • Her wide eyes, filled with hope and happiness, stared at the shoreline.（真光卷原文）
  • He glanced over his shoulder nervously.
  • All eyes were fixed on...

四、环境呼应句库（开头结尾各一套）

  • As the afternoon sun bathed the beach with warm, golden rays...（真光卷原文开篇）
  • The sun began to set, painting the sky with shades of orange.（真光卷续写段首句）
  • Laughter lingered in the air long after the party ended.
  • The gentle breeze carried the scent of ... , as if cheering for us.

五、升华点题句库

  • It was not the party itself, but the love from strangers, that made the day unforgettable.
  • Kindness, once given, always finds its way back.
  • From that day on, he understood that ...（成长类）
  • Sometimes, a small act of kindness can light up someone's whole world.
