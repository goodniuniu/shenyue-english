# 应用文万能句库（作文语料库 · 只增不减）

> 用途：应用文写作（报道/邮件/发言稿三大家族）的句型弹药库。每次考试遇到新的好句子、范文金句，追加到对应分区，**只增不减**。
> 用法：写应用文时每类挑 2–3 句套用，避免堆砌；配合[应用文写作模板与高级句型](../../典型题型与方法/高二深化_英语_典型题型与方法_应用文写作模板与高级句型.md)使用。

## 一、开头句（身份/背景 + 目的）

| 场景 | 万能句 |
|------|--------|
| 邮件-请求 | I'm writing to ask for your guidance on..., which has long puzzled us. |
| 邮件-邀请 | On behalf of our class, I sincerely invite you to participate in... |
| 发言稿 | Good morning, everyone. It's my great honor to share with you our experience of... |
| 报道-导语 | Aiming to broaden our horizons, our school launched a (n) ... activity last Friday. |
| 报道-导语 | An English debate on "..." was successfully held in our school hall, attracting over 100 students. |

## 二、主体句（过程 + 亮点）

| 功能 | 万能句 |
|------|--------|
| 列要点 | First and foremost, ... / What's more, ... / Last but not least, ... |
| 描述过程（非谓语开头） | Guided by our teacher, we divided the task into three parts. |
| 描述反响（被动包装） | The activity was warmly received and highly praised by both teachers and students. |
| 强调亮点 | It was the teamwork that made our project stand out. |
| with 复合 | With everyone's efforts combined, we finally achieved a satisfying result. |
| 数据支撑 | According to the feedback, over 80% of the participants found the activity rewarding. |

## 三、观点/感想句（辩论、展示、报道通用）

| 功能 | 万能句 |
|------|--------|
| 个人观点 | From my perspective, AI should serve as a tool rather than a substitute for our own thinking. |
| 双面平衡 | Every coin has two sides; the key lies in how we make use of it. |
| 收获体会 | What I gained from the activity was not only knowledge but also the spirit of cooperation. |
| 倒装升级 | Only in this way can we strike a balance between study and technology. |
| 虚拟语气 | Without this hands-on experience, we would never have understood... |

## 四、结尾句（感谢/期待/呼吁）

| 场景 | 万能句 |
|------|--------|
| 邮件收尾 | I would appreciate it if you could offer us some practical suggestions. |
| 邮件收尾 | Looking forward to your early reply. |
| 发言稿收尾 | That's all for my sharing. Thank you for your attention. |
| 报道收尾 | The activity not only enriched our campus life but also planted a seed of... in our hearts. |

## 五、加分替换表（阅卷"高级感"）

| 普通写法 | 高级替换 |
|----------|----------|
| I think | From my perspective / As far as I'm concerned |
| very important | of great significance / plays a vital role in |
| many students | a large number of / the majority of students |
| good | rewarding / fruitful / splendid |
| We should | It is high time that we... / We are supposed to |
| more and more | an increasing number of |
| finally | eventually / ultimately |

## 六、话题金句（按月考高频话题积累）

### 科技与学习（真光卷辩论赛原型）

- While AI assists learning, independent thinking remains irreplaceable.
- Technology is a double-edged sword; what truly matters is how we wield it.

### 中国文化与科技自信（语法填空三篇话题延伸）

- Rooted in a history of over 600 years, the Palace Museum now embraces the digital age.
- From karez wells to HarmonyOS, Chinese wisdom never stops innovating.

### 环境保护（阅读高频话题延伸）

- Every small green action counts toward a sustainable future.
- Only by living in harmony with nature can we build a better home.
